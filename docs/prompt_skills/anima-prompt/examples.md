# Anima提示词示例

## 示例1：单角色

**输入：**
黄昏时分的日式庭院，枫叶飘落，柔和的夕阳光线从侧面照来，地面铺着石板小径，旁边有一盏石灯笼，氛围宁静温暖。一位少女单膝跪在石板地面上，身体微微前倾，右膝着地支撑，左腿向前屈起脚掌踩地，右手轻抚搭在左膝上，左手自然垂落在身侧，面朝斜前方微微仰头，目光远眺，嘴角带着淡淡笑意。她有着银白色齐肩短发，左侧发间生有细长的白色狐耳，身穿素白色和服腰系淡紫色宽腰带，脚穿白色足袋，左脚踝处系着一条细银铃铛脚链。

**输出：**
masterpiece, best quality, score_9, highres, newest, year 2025, safe, 1girl. Japanese garden at dusk, maple leaves falling, soft sunset light from the side, stone pavement path, stone lantern nearby, tranquil warm atmosphere. A girl kneels on the stone ground on her right knee with body slightly leaning forward, left leg bent forward and foot flat on the ground, right hand resting gently on her left knee, left arm hanging naturally at her side, facing diagonally forward with head tilted slightly up and gazing into distance with a faint smile. She has silver white shoulder length hair with exclusive slender white (fox ears:2) on the left side of her head, and is wearing a plain white kimono with soft purple wide obi sash, white tabi socks, and a thin silver bell anklet on her left ankle. Serene and warm evening atmosphere.

**注意：** 单人（`1girl`）输出末尾不加`Each character has own separate head, face, body`。

## 示例2：多角色

**输入：**
精致典雅的古风动漫场景，云雾缭绕、缥缈清幽的山间仙境，四周草木繁茂葱郁，柔和温润的自然光倾泻而下，淡淡的林间薄雾缓缓飘散，氛围静谧宛若仙境，画风细腻柔和，尽显清幽雅致的古典意境。有一个少女趴在一个萝莉身上，四目相对，均呈现完整全身身形。下面的萝莉长着黑色长发安然躺卧在繁花盛放的地面，身着素雅简约的纯白色连衣裙，搭配整洁的白色长袜，神情恬淡静静休憩，面部朝上，她没有狐耳和龙尾。上面的少女长着白色长发，双手撑在下方萝莉身体两侧支撑身躯，双腿分置在下方萝莉腿部两侧，生有蓬松柔软的雪白狐耳，修长顺滑带鳞纹的龙尾自后腰自然垂落，五官温婉精致，眼眸灵动柔和，身着轻盈雅致的汉服，双腿穿着细腻顺滑的白色连裤袜，身姿柔美曼妙。整体画面静谧悠然，人物姿态自然舒展。

**输出：**
masterpiece, best quality, score_9, highres, newest, year 2025, safe, 2girls. Ancient Chinese mountain fairyland, misty clouds, lush vegetation, warm natural light, faint forest mist, serene atmosphere, elegant art style. There are two characters, both are full body portraits, facing each other with eyes meeting. The girl on top is lying on top of the loli below and leaning over from above, propping her body up with hands on both sides of the loli and legs straddling the loli's sides. She has long white hair with exclusive fluffy soft white (fox ears:2) and exclusive slender smooth (dragon tail:2) with scale patterns naturally hanging from her lower back, gentle delicate facial features and lively soft eyes, and is wearing a light elegant hanfu with white pantyhose, with a graceful slender figure. The loli on bottom is lying on the flower-covered ground on her back with face up and arms resting at her sides. She has long black hair and does NOT have fox ears or dragon tail, is wearing a simple white dress with white stockings, and has a calm expression as she rests peacefully. Each character has own separate head, face, body. Serene and leisurely atmosphere, naturally stretched postures.

**注意：** 多人（`2girls`）输出末尾加`Each character has own separate head, face, body`。否定特征（`does NOT have fox ears or dragon tail`）绑定到对应角色。
