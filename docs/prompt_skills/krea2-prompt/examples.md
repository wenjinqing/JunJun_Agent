# Krea 2 提示词示例

## 示例 1：插画风格单人

**输入：** 黄昏时分的日式庭院，一位银白短发少女单膝跪在石板地面上，身穿素白色和服腰系淡紫色腰带，白色狐耳狐尾，面带淡淡微笑眺望远方，枫叶飘落，柔和夕阳光线从侧面照来。

**输出：**
> A stylized digital painting of a petite young woman with silver-white shoulder-length hair kneeling on one knee on a stone path in a Japanese garden at dusk. A pair of white fox ears protrude from her hair and a matching fluffy tail curls behind her. She wears a pure white kimono with a pale lavender obi, a small silver bell tied to her left ankle. She gazes into the distance with a gentle smile as maple leaves drift down around her. Soft golden sunset light streams in from the side, casting warm long shadows across the scene. Medium shot at eye level with a shallow depth of field.

## 示例 2：写实风格室内

**输入：** 一位年轻女性坐在咖啡厅窗边，黑色长发，穿着米白色毛衣，手里捧着一杯热气腾腾的咖啡，窗外的午后阳光照在她脸上，表情安静放松。

**输出：**
> A photograph of a young woman with long straight black hair sitting by a window in a cozy café, wearing a soft cream-colored sweater. She holds a steaming cup of coffee in both hands, a quiet and relaxed expression on her face. Warm afternoon sunlight streams through the window, casting soft highlights across her hair and illuminating the gentle steam rising from the cup. Medium close-up portrait with a natural depth of field, the background dissolves into a soft bokeh of blurred café interior. Soft diffused natural lighting with warm golden tones.

---

## 示例 3：多人场景

**输入：** 两个少女在图书馆的书架间面对面站立，一人金发蓝眼穿白色衬衫，另一人黑发绿眼穿黑色连衣裙，两人正在交谈。

**输出：**
> A stylized illustration of two young women standing face to face between tall bookshelves in a quiet library. In the foreground, a blonde woman with bright blue eyes wears a crisp white button-down shirt, one hand resting on a bookshelf as she leans slightly forward. Behind her, a second woman with long black hair and striking green eyes stands with her arms loosely crossed, wearing a simple black dress. Soft warm light filters through a nearby window, casting gentle shadows across the wooden floor and the rows of books. Medium shot at eye level with moderate depth of field, muted earthy color palette with warm amber undertones.
